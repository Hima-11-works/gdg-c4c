The Problem & Urgency

Macro Blindspots & Fragmented Climate Action

Why traditional ambient monitoring fails episodic, hyper-local pollution surges in India
THE CHALLENGE

🛑 The Sparse Sensor Reality

India operates ~500 official CAAQMS stations for 1.4 billion people. Stations are clustered in metro centers (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru), leaving peri-urban, rural agricultural, and industrial corridors unmonitored.

🌫️ Unmonitored Episodic Surges

  • Agricultural Stubble Burning across Punjab, Haryana, and Western UP occurs between stations.
  • Unregistered Brick Kilns & Industrial Bypasses dump emissions during nocturnal thermal inversions.
  • Landfill Fires create toxic dioxin plumes that disperse before macro stations register spikes.

🚧 Inter-Agency Jurisdictional Silos

Air pollution respects no administrative boundaries. Wind transports particulate plumes across state lines, yet regional authorities (DPCC, PPCB, UPPCB) cannot share raw citizen reports or sensor data due to privacy and jurisdiction walls.

⏱️ The Critical 48-Hour Inaction Gap

Macro station alerts often arrive 24–48 hours after an emission surge has dispersed into residential communities, rendering municipal emergency intervention reactive rather than preventive.

Core Capability 1

Autonomous Hidden Hotspot Detection

Corroborating satellite screening anomalies with ground truth
SPACE + GROUND FUSION

🛰️ Satellite Anomaly Screening

Copernicus Sentinel-5P UV Aerosol Index (UVAI) passes detect elevated absorbing aerosols. When UVAI breaches threshold values, an autonomous Hotspot Candidate is generated on the corresponding H3 cell.

🔥 Multi-Sensor Cross-Corroboration

A single satellite pixel can be distorted by cloud reflection. The engine searches downwind and adjacent cells for:

  • NASA FIRMS VIIRS Pixels: 375m thermal fire anomaly detections.
  • Citizen Smoke Reports: Geotagged plume submissions with EXIF-free photos.
  • Community Micro-Sensors: Particulate spikes in low-cost PM2.5 monitors.

🎯 Confidence Scoring & False-Alarm Rejection

Candidates are evaluated against a multi-factor confidence scoring algorithm (0.00 – 1.00). High-confidence candidates trigger an immediate inspection ticket in the authority review console, preventing false dispatch alarms.

🧑‍💼 Human-in-the-Loop Moderation

Municipal officers inspect the corroborated evidence packet, satellite tile, and Gemini image assessment before approving field intervention orders.

Core Capability 2

Economic Corridor Forecasting & Routing

Spatio-temporal exposure modeling along India's major freight arteries
LOGISTICS & RESILIENCE

🛣️ National Economic Corridors

  • Delhi–Kanpur Interstate Corridor (NH-19 / Indo-Gangetic Plain axis).
  • DMIC (Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor) connecting NCR, Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Maharashtra.
  • EDFC (Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor) from Sahnewal to Dankuni.

⏰ 1h, 3h, 6h Forward Projection

Using physical advection-dispersion modeling driven by Open-Meteo wind fields ($u/v$ vectors) and boundary layer heights, the platform models particulate transport along transport corridors.

🚛 Exposure-Aware Logistics Routing

Logistics operators and fleet managers identify cleaner transit windows to avoid severe winter temperature inversions, reducing fleet driver occupational exposure and freight transit delays.

📊 Strictly Honest Evaluation

Corridor forecasts are scored against real withheld ground stations using RMSE and MAE metrics. The platform strictly rejects synthetic score inflation or claiming unvalidated accuracy.

Core Capability 5

Inter-Agency Federated Climate Intelligence

Collaborative AI modeling across states while preserving data sovereignty
FEDERATED ML

🏛️ The Inter-State Challenge

Air pollution crosses state boundaries freely, but state pollution control boards (Delhi DPCC, Punjab PPCB, Haryana SPCB) operate under strict legal and jurisdictional silos and cannot pool raw citizen data or proprietary factory sensor registries.

🔒 Privacy-Preserving Architecture

  • Local Training: Each regional node trains localized ridge-residual calibration models on its private local data.
  • Cryptographic Weight Exchange: Only signed model parameter weights are transmitted to the central aggregator over HTTP.
  • 100% Data Sovereignty: Raw sensor measurements and citizen reports never leave the local node's perimeter.

⚙️ Working Multi-Party CLI Tooling

Ships with fully operational aggregator and client command-line interfaces:

python -m app.federation_aggregator --port 8010
python -m app.federation_client --participant delhi
python -m app.federation_client --participant punjab

Includes a two-partition local simulation mode (`app.cli federation-demo`) testable in seconds.
